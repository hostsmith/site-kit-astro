The starter that raises every rye loaf we bake was first fed in September 2001. It has lived through two ovens, one move and a very cold winter.

## What makes it rye

- **Flour**: whole rye from a farm in Järva county
- **Time**: 16 hours of rising
- **Salt and water**: nothing else

To celebrate, every rye loaf this week comes with a jar of starter to take home.
