A budget that holds starts before you call a contractor. These four steps work for a $6,000 bathroom refresh and a $60,000 kitchen alike.

## 1. Write down what the project is for

"More counter space and a dishwasher" is a goal you can price. "A new kitchen" isn't. A short list of goals keeps the scope, and the budget, from growing on its own.

## 2. Get prices for the big items first

Cabinets, countertops, tile and fixtures make up most of a kitchen or bathroom budget. Price them before anything else, and you'll know early whether the plan fits.

## 3. Keep a reserve of 10 to 15 percent

Older houses hide surprises behind the walls: old wiring, a rotten subfloor, a vent that goes nowhere. A reserve means a surprise is a decision, not a crisis.

## 4. Decide where to save

Spend on what you touch every day, such as faucets, drawers and the counter. Save on what you can upgrade later, such as lighting and hardware.
