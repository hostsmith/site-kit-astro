Most of our customers stay at home while we remodel their bathroom. With a little planning, the three to four weeks go by with fewer surprises than you'd expect.

## Before we start

- **Plan your mornings.** If it's your only bathroom, ask a neighbor or the gym, or rent a portable shower for the two weeks the room has no plumbing.
- **Clear the room.** Take out everything from the cabinets and the closet next door. Dust finds its way into anything left behind.
- **Settle the pets.** Cats in particular like to inspect an empty tub. Keep them in another room while the crew works.

## While the work goes on

We seal off the work area with zip walls and cover the floors from the front door to the bathroom. At the end of every day the site is swept and the tools are put away.

Your project lead sends a short update every few days, and you can call them any time with a question.

## The last week

Tile needs time to cure and grout needs time to set. The room may look finished a few days before it's ready to use. We'll tell you exactly when you can take the first shower.
