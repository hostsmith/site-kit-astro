Minnesota winters find every weak spot in a house. These jobs take an afternoon in September or October, and most need nothing more than a screwdriver and a caulk gun.

## Outside

- **Gutters and downspouts:** clear the leaves so melting snow drains away from the foundation instead of freezing on the roof edge.
- **Hose bibs:** disconnect the garden hoses and shut off the inside valves, so the pipes behind them can't freeze and split.
- **Caulk:** check around windows and doors, and replace any that is cracked or pulling away.

## Inside

- **Door sweeps and weatherstripping:** if you can see daylight under a door, you're heating the porch.
- **Furnace filter:** change it before the first cold night, and book a service if it hasn't had one in two years.
- **Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors:** test them and change the batteries.
