Two quotes for the same bathroom can differ by thousands of dollars. Usually it isn't that one contractor is cheaper: it's that one quote leaves things out. Here's what to look for before you sign.

## Scope, written down

A good quote lists every task: removal, disposal, prep, installation and finishing. If a line just says "bathroom renovation", ask for the breakdown. Check that it covers:

- materials named by brand or spec, or a clear allowance amount;
- who handles permits and inspections;
- clean-up and waste removal;
- a start date and an expected duration.

> If it isn't in writing, assume it isn't included.

## Red flags

Be cautious of large upfront deposits, quotes that expire within a day, or a contractor who won't say how changes are priced. In Minnesota, ask for the contractor's state license number and look it up before you pay anything.

## Comparing two quotes

Put the quotes side by side, line by line. Where one quote has a line the other doesn't, ask the second contractor whether it's included. Most of the price gap usually closes once both quotes cover the same work.
